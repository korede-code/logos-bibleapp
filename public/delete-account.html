🗑️ Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

📱 Method 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open the Synthesis Bible app
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Go to Settings (gear icon)
    4. Tap "🗑️ Delete My Account & Data"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

📧 Method 2: Email Request

  1. Send an email to: synthesisbible@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. In the email body, include:
    • Your registered email address
    • Your display name (if known)
    • Reason for deletion (optional - helps us improve)
📧 Send Deletion Email

📋 What Data Will Be Deleted

  • ✅ Your account profile (name, email, photo)
  • ✅ All saved notes and highlights
  • ✅ All bookmarks and reading history
  • ✅ Prayer journal entries
  • ✅ Reading plan progress
  • ✅ Usage statistics and streaks
  • ✅ All personal preferences and settings

📌 What Data Is Retained

  • Anonymized usage statistics (cannot be linked to you)
  • Payment transaction records (required by law for tax purposes)

⏱️ Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently deleted.

If you change your mind within 7 days, contact us to cancel the deletion request.
