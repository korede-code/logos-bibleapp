Our Promise

Privacy-first. No data selling. No third-party ads. No tracking.

We collect account information (name, email), user content (notes, highlights, prayers), and anonymous usage data. We DO NOT collect location data, browsing history, or contacts.

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Synthesis Bible service. We use Firebase for authentication and data storage.

All data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

We use Firebase for authentication and data storage, and Google Text-to-Speech for audio features.

Your Rights

You can access, export, or delete your data anytime in the app Settings.

Request Account Deletion

How to delete your account and data:

  1. In-App: Go to Settings → Scroll to bottom → Tap "Delete Account"
  2. Email Request: Send an email to the address below with subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email address.

What gets deleted:

  • Your account (email, name, profile photo)
  • All notes, highlights, and bookmarks
  • Prayer journal entries
  • Reading history and progress

Timeline: Data is deleted within 7 business days of request.
